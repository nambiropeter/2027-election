About KPolls Kenya

KPolls Kenya is an independent public sentiment poll focused on transparent, lightweight, and accessible participation around major national issues.

What this poll is

  • A snapshot of public preference from participating users.
  • A live tally of submitted responses.
  • A civic engagement tool, not an official election instrument.

How fairness is protected

  • Kenya-focused geolocation checks.
  • One-vote-per-device controls.
  • Rate limiting and anomaly detection against abuse.

Editorial note

Candidate information is presented for context and may evolve over time.
