Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

KPolls Kenya collects only the minimum data required to operate this election preference poll, prevent abuse, and provide trustworthy aggregated results.

What we collect

  • Voting choice and timestamp.
  • Hashed device/session identifiers used for one-vote-per-device enforcement.
  • Hashed IP-based identifiers for anti-abuse controls and rate limits.
  • Country code determined from IP geolocation to enforce Kenya-only voting.

Cookies and local storage

  • Essential session cookies are used to issue and validate vote sessions.
  • Local storage stores your advertising consent choice.
  • Advertising cookies may be used by Google AdSense when you allow ads.

How we use data

  • To process votes and publish aggregate poll totals.
  • To detect and prevent suspicious voting behavior.
  • To operate, monitor, and secure the service.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We use third-party processors for infrastructure, analytics, and advertising delivery where applicable.

Your choices

  • You can choose whether ads are enabled through the consent prompt.
  • You may contact us to request policy clarification or legal requests.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, use the contact page.

KPolls Kenya is an independent civic sentiment poll and is not an official election instrument.
